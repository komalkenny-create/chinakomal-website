North American Unconventional Activity Drives Demand for Workover Equipment, High-Pressure Mud Pumps and Solids Control

September 30, 2026

BEIJING, China – CHINA KOMAL INTERNATIONAL CO., LTD reports growing engagement across North America's onshore oil and gas market, as operators and service companies sustain infill drilling, refracturing and artificial-lift programmes across the continent's major unconventional basins. According to industry analysis, activity in the region is being shaped by two converging forces: a mature, high-density development model that keeps drilling and workover crews busy through the year, and sustained capital discipline that puts uptime, parts availability and cost per barrel at the centre of every procurement decision.

Operators working the Permian, Bakken, Eagle Ford, Haynesville and Appalachian plays are managing large, mature producing portfolios with high well counts per pad. Infill drilling, secondary recovery, refracturing and routine well intervention generate continuous demand for the fast-moving equipment and consumables that keep rigs and well-servicing units running: fluid end parts, shaker screens and dependable high-pressure pumping capacity.

"North America is the most demanding aftermarket in the world," said CHINA KOMAL's regional sales director for North America. "The wells are numerous, the decline curves are steep, and the operating model rewards speed and predictability. When a workover crew is on location, every hour of downtime is a deferred barrel and a stranded rig. Contractors here don't buy parts—they buy uptime, documented to specification and available when the truck rolls."

Mature, High-Density Fields Keep Workover and Intervention Demand Firm

North America's unconventional fields are, by global standards, mature and densely developed. With decline rates that require constant intervention to hold production, operators run workover rigs, coiled tubing units and well-servicing crews continuously. That rhythm drives steady consumption of matched fluid end kits—pistons, cylinder liners, valve bodies and seats, and packings for scheduled pump rebuilds—as well as shaker screens for the shale shakers, mud cleaners and decanter centrifuges that anchor solids control on land rigs and workover units.

CHINA KOMAL reports growing orders for its composite-frame shaker screen line, covering Brandt (B40, D380, King Cobra, LCM-2D) and Derrick (DP-600, FLC-2000, Hyperpool) configurations among others. Because operators in the region tend to standardise on a limited set of screen types to simplify logistics and crew training, screen supply has become a focus of North American inquiries.

"On a mature unconventional field, the operating budget lives and dies on run-time," said CHINA KOMAL's solids control engineering lead. "Customers here want screens and pump parts with predictable cut points and a supply schedule they can plan around in advance. When you are managing a high well count and a full workover calendar, an unplanned change-out on a pump or a shaker is not just a maintenance line item—it is a deferred barrel and a crew standing idle."

Refracturing and Deeper Workover Push Higher-Specification Pumping

Alongside production support, refracturing, plug-and-perf work, milling and wellbore cleanout operations are raising demand for dependable, higher-specification pumping equipment. High-rate stimulation support, cementing and displacement work all depend on high-pressure pumping capacity that performs predictably across long, cost-sensitive campaigns where a single equipment failure can mean costly rig standby and missed completion windows.

CHINA KOMAL has recorded increasing interest in its KM series high-pressure mud pumps for drilling, workover and intervention applications, available across a range of power ratings to match different rig and unit classes. The pumps are being specified alongside the company's fluid end parts programme covering widely used triplex and quintuplex configurations, including parts matching Gardner Denver PZ and TGE series pumps as well as IDECO, OILWELL and EMSCO platforms common across the region's service fleet.

"In a refract or a deep workover, the equipment spec is set before the crew moves in, and a failure at depth costs days, not hours," the regional sales director explained. "Operators and contractors in North America treat part quality as a risk-management decision rather than a line-item cost, which is driving a clear shift toward certified components with full material traceability and repeatable performance."

Solids Control Economics in Tight and Abrasive Formations

North America's varied geology—reactive shales, high-clay sections, depleted pressure windows and abrasive formations—places particular demands on solids control. Effective screen selection, together with mud cleaner and centrifuge performance, is central to drilling efficiency, mud costs and waste volumes, and CHINA KOMAL's regional team pairs screen recommendations with cuttings analysis at the start of each campaign so that contractors can match mesh and screen type to the formation rather than defaulting to a single specification.

According to CHINA KOMAL's engineering team, the priority for customers in the region is not simply availability but the assurance that a replacement part will perform to specification for the full service interval—an expectation that has shaped the company's inspection and quality-control procedures. Composite-frame screens with documented mesh counts and consistent conductance are a particular focus.

Regional Stocking, Support and Lifecycle Partnerships

To serve a market that stretches from the Permian Basin to the Appalachian and Bakken plays, CHINA KOMAL is strengthening its North American logistics network, maintaining buffer inventory of high-turnover items including fluid end parts, shaker screens and common pump spares at stocking points with road and rail access to major operating centres in Texas, Oklahoma, North Dakota, Pennsylvania and Ohio. The company reports that standard replacement items can be dispatched from regional stock within days of order confirmation, with application support available remotely and on-site for rig-up, commissioning and solids control optimisation.

The company is also working with North American service companies to build local maintenance capability, providing training on pump inspection, fluid end rebuild procedures and screen selection practices. CHINA KOMAL says the objective is to help operators and contractors stay self-sufficient between major overhauls, with the regional team serving as a technical backstop rather than a dependency—an approach proven across its expanding international operations.

"The companies winning in North America are those that treat supply chain planning as seriously as drilling planning," the regional sales director concluded. "From mature-field workover campaigns to refract and deep-workover programmes, our job is to put world-class pumps, parts and screens in our customers' hands and back them with inventory and expertise that match the scale of their operations. In a market this demanding, uptime is the product—and that is what we deliver."
