Southeast Asia Drilling and Geothermal Expansion Drives Demand for High-Temperature Solids Control and Mud Pump Parts

August 13, 2026

BEIJING, China – CHINA KOMAL INTERNATIONAL CO., LTD reports rising demand from Southeast Asia for solids control equipment, replacement shaker screens, and mud pump wear parts, as the region's offshore drilling programs recover and its geothermal sector accelerates. According to industry analysis, Indonesia, Malaysia, and Vietnam are simultaneously advancing offshore development campaigns and geothermal power expansion, creating a diversified equipment market with distinct technical requirements—most notably high-temperature drilling fluid handling.

The demand pattern reflects two converging trends. In the offshore sector, operators are returning to development drilling across established basins, with rig utilization climbing and well-servicing contractors upgrading older fleets. In the geothermal sector, national utilities and independent developers are pushing ahead with new wellfield programs, drilling deeper and hotter wells that place unusual stress on mud handling systems. Industry observers note that geothermal drilling fluids routinely circulate at temperatures well above those seen in conventional oil and gas wells, which is forcing operators to reassess the thermal limits of their solids control packages.

"Southeast Asia is no longer a single story—it is a set of parallel stories with one common theme: heat and abrasion," said CHINA KOMAL's regional sales director for Southeast Asia. "Offshore wells in the region are abrasive and high-angle, while geothermal wells are genuinely hot. Both punish mud handling equipment, and both are driving operators toward solids control systems that are built for sustained duty rather than intermittent use."

High-Temperature Solids Control for Geothermal Wells

CHINA KOMAL has recorded growing specification of its solids control line for geothermal drilling programs across Indonesia and the Philippines, where operators are drilling replacement and step-out wells to sustain existing power plants and feed new ones. Geothermal drilling demands continuous circulation management under high temperatures, with cuttings removal, solids settling, and fluid conditioning performed around the clock. The company's shale shakers, mud cleaners, desanders, desilters, and decanter centrifuges are increasingly configured as integrated packages for these rigs, with high-temp elastomer options and heavy-duty screening decks specified for the abrasive, high-solids cuttings typical of volcanic formations.

Replacement shaker screens have become a particular focus, as geothermal cuttings quickly wear out conventional mesh. CHINA KOMAL's composite-frame screen line—covering Brandt (B40, D380, King Cobra, LCM-2D) and Derrick (DP-600, FLC-2000, Hyperpool) configurations, among others—is being specified for its steel-reinforced polyurethane frames and consistent tensioning under elevated fluid temperatures. According to the company's solids control engineering team, screen selection for geothermal applications emphasizes open-screen area and mesh durability over raw throughput, because downtime for screen changes on a geothermal well carries outsized cost.

"In geothermal drilling, every screen change means circulating without full solids removal, and every hour of that costs the operator money in lost drilling progress," explained CHINA KOMAL's director of solids control engineering. "Our engineering support for these projects starts with cuttings analysis and ends with a screen and centrifuge configuration matched to the actual formation. That discipline is what operators in the region are asking for."

Mud Pump Parts Demand Tracks Fleet Utilization

Alongside solids control, CHINA KOMAL has seen steady growth in orders for mud pump fluid end parts from Southeast Asian drilling contractors and rig owners. As offshore rig utilization recovers, operators are working pumps harder and scheduling more frequent preventive maintenance, driving demand for piston assemblies, cylinder liners, valve bodies and seats, and intermediate rods. The company's parts program covers widely used triplex and quintuplex configurations, including Gardner Denver PZ and TGE series pumps, as well as IDECO, OILWELL, and EMSCO platform designs common in the region's rig fleet.

CHINA KOMAL's KM series high-pressure pumps continue to be specified on new-build rig packages and major refurbishment projects across the region, where contractors value the series' simplified maintenance access and interchangeable wear parts. According to the company, the KM pump family—available in a range of power ratings to match different rig classes—is increasingly paired with regional buffer inventory of the most frequently ordered fluid end parts, shortening lead times for contractors operating far from major service hubs.

"Our customers in Southeast Asia run lean maintenance teams and long supply chains," the regional sales director said. "They need parts that arrive quickly, fit without modification, and survive the local conditions. That is exactly the position our fluid end parts program and KM pump line are designed to fill, and it is why we continue to build regional stock and application support."

Regional Support Infrastructure Expands

To support the growing installed base, CHINA KOMAL has strengthened its Southeast Asian logistics and technical support network, maintaining buffer inventory of the most frequently ordered shaker screens and mud pump parts at regional stocking points, with application support available remotely and on-site for rig-up, commissioning, and solids control optimization. The company reports that standard replacement items can be shipped from stock within days of order confirmation.

"Southeast Asia is one of the most diversified equipment markets in the world—offshore, onshore, and geothermal running side by side—and it rewards suppliers who can serve all three from a single regional platform," the regional sales director concluded. "With our complete solids control line, shaker screen program, KM series pumps, and fluid end parts for the industry's most common pump platforms, we are positioned to support the region's operators as drilling and geothermal activity continue to build through 2027."
